Receiving rules

  • Full receipt: Confirmed positive carton count equals PO quantity with no conflict.
  • Partial receipt: Confirmed positive carton count is lower than PO quantity with no conflict.
  • Hold: Zero count, overage (> PO), missing physical count, unit mismatch (other or unknown), or unresolved disagreement/conflict.
  • Count scope: Superseded historical counts and declared/driver estimates are excluded; independent non-overlapping stacks are summed into one total.
  • Operational boundary: Separates AI extraction, operator confirmation, and policy decision support. Never auto-accepts receipts or mutates inventory.
